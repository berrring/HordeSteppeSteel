# One-minute Checkpoint 1 demonstration

Open `HordeSteppeSteel.rbxl` in Roblox Studio and press **Play**. Begin the capture on the Horde main menu. Use a wide viewport if possible. If the inspector covers a map marker, close it with **×**. WASD, the scroll wheel, and **F** help reframe the map.

| Time | What to show |
| --- | --- |
| 0–10 s | Show the main menu and click **New Campaign → Start Campaign**. |
| 10–20 s | Show the 12-province board, both faction colors and the 3D miniatures. |
| 20–35 s | Click the gold **LC 17** at **Urgench**, then the teal **Kipchak Steppe** destination. Show the march and the spent order. |
| 35–45 s | Click the gold **HA 15** at **Sarai**, then the red **IN 5** at **Kiev**. Show Mongol and Rus losses and the Kiev capture. This uses the intentionally weakened frontier detachment in the fixed opening. |
| 45–55 s | Click **End Turn**. The turn indicator changes to **Rus turn** and the end-turn control disables during resolution. |
| 55–60 s | Show Rus action feedback and **Turn 2 · Your turn**. Point out that income was credited and surviving Mongol armies can act again. |

Rehearse once before recording. Start a fresh campaign for the actual take so the army positions and losses match this sequence.
