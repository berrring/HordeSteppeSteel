# Horde: Steppe & Steel

Horde: Steppe & Steel is Team Arsenal's stylized historical strategy game set across medieval Eurasia. The active prototype runs in Roblox Studio.

## Play the Roblox campaign

See [Roblox setup and controls](Roblox/README.md) for the native Studio package. In Play mode, choose **New campaign** and select a 12, 14, or 16 tile map. Lead the Mongol Khanate from Sarai, cross at a ford, and capture Novgorod before the Rus take Sarai.

The Mongol Khanate is playable and the Rus' Principalities are the AI opponent. Cumans / Kipchaks, Byzantine Empire, Seljuk Sultanate, and Khwarazmshah Dynasty remain planned civilizations.

The official palette is Steppe Green (#6B8E23), Wood Brown (#5C4033), Bronze Gold (#DAA520), Snow White (#E8ECEF), and Deep Steel Blue (#2B3A4A). The original showcase and isolated mounted archer remain included as art references.
